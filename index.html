INSIGHTS

How can AI help my business?

A plain-English guide to where AI can help a business, how to start with one low-risk task, and where AI is not the right answer.

Book a Strategy CallAll Services

The short answer

AI helps most with repetitive, information-heavy work: answering common questions, sorting enquiries, summarising documents and drafting routine content.

Where to start

Pick one frequent, low-risk task. Measure how long it takes today, test a small solution, then expand only if it works.

Where AI is not the answer

If a process is broken or unclear, fix the process first. Simple automation is often cheaper and more reliable than AI.

Keep people in charge

Use AI to assist, with human review for anything important, and be careful with private data.

What are AI agents?

What AI agents are, how they differ from chatbots, and how a business can use them safely with limited permissions and human oversight.

Book a Strategy CallAll Services

Definition

An AI agent is software that uses AI to carry out multi-step tasks, often by using tools such as email, calendars or databases.

Agents versus chatbots

A chatbot mostly answers questions. An agent can also take actions, which means permissions and oversight matter more.

Using them safely

Limit access, log what the agent does, test before launch and keep a human approval step for important actions.

FAQ
What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is software that greets visitors or callers, answers routine questions, collects details and supports bookings, passing complex requests to a person.

Can an AI receptionist replace a human receptionist?

Usually not entirely. It handles repetitive enquiries well, while people should handle complex, sensitive or high-value conversations.

Does an AI receptionist answer phone calls?

It can where voice technology and telephone integration are suitable. Many businesses start with website chat and messaging; we confirm what is feasible during discovery.

Is it suitable for clinics or healthcare?

It can help with administrative tasks such as opening hours and booking requests, but it must not give medical advice, and patient data needs careful privacy planning.
